Talk to your computer.
Even without internet.

Axor is a local AI voice assistant. It opens apps, sets reminders, schedules meetings and sends messages, in English and Hindi, and syncs to the cloud when you are back online.

See what it does Features

What it does

Offline first

Speech recognition (Whisper) and the command engine run on your machine.

Voice and text

Type for a text reply, speak for a spoken one.

Hindi + English

Understands Hinglish commands like "youtube kholo".

Smart sync

Queues your data offline and syncs when connected.

Dashboard

History, analytics, calendar, reminders and notes on the web.

Connects to your tools

Gmail, Google Calendar, Firebase, Supabase, Cloudinary (optional, with your keys).
